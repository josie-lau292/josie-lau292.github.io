title: A Gentle Introduction to Probability
excerpt: Probability is not a prediction machine—it is a language for uncertainty.
date: '2026-01-24'
readTime: 4 min read
category: statistics
---

Probability describes how likely an outcome is, on a scale from 0 to 1. A probability of 0 means an outcome cannot happen; 1 means it is certain.

For a fair coin, the probability of heads is 0.5. This does not promise heads on the next toss. It means that over many tosses, heads should occur about half the time.

That distinction matters. Probability is about patterns across repeated opportunities, not certainty in a single moment. It helps us make thoughtful decisions when we cannot know exactly what will happen next.

When working with probability, say the event out loud first. Clear language usually makes the calculation clearer too.

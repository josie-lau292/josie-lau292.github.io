The **mean** tells us where scores tend to sit. Standard deviation tells us how much they tend to spread out around that mean.

If most scores cluster closely together, the standard deviation is small. If scores are more scattered, it is large. Neither is inherently better: they simply describe different patterns.

Imagine two tutorial groups with the same average mark. One group’s marks are all fairly similar; the other has a mix of very high and very low marks. Their means match, but their standard deviations do not.

Standard deviation gives the average a little context. Before interpreting a number as “typical”, it helps to know how much variation is sitting behind it.
